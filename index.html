      Reading these numbers

      • Forecast values come directly from Open-Meteo weather models for the hour you're estimated to arrive. Wind is the model's 10 m wind, not wind at handlebar height.
      • Estimate and calculated values are RideWind's own arithmetic: arrival times assume a constant average speed, and the headwind and crosswind components come from that forecast wind and the route's direction.
      • Forecast wind does not fully account for shelter from buildings, trees, hedges, cuttings or local terrain. Exposed roads may feel stronger wind, and sheltered ones weaker.
      • Headwind means the wind is within 45° of straight on, tailwind within 45° of directly behind, and anything else is crosswind. These are rough planning guides, not race advice.
      • The power estimate uses a published steady-state cycling model (Martin et al., 1998) with your settings, gradient, air density and the wind along your direction of travel. It ignores stops, traffic, corners, accelerations and fatigue, so treat it as an approximation. It has not been validated against your own rides and is not a race prediction.

      Your data stays in this browser

      Settings and saved routes are stored only in this browser on this device. RideWind has no accounts and no server, so nothing syncs between devices. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them. To move data to another device, export it here and import it there.

      Export files contain your saved routes. Treat them as personal, because routes can reveal where you live or ride.
